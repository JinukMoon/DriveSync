ScreenSync

A small personal Android app that uploads new screenshots to the user's own Google Drive.

What it does

  • Watches one folder on the phone chosen by the user (by default DCIM/Screenshots).
  • When a new file appears there, uploads it, under its original file name, to a single folder in the user's own Google Drive.
  • One-way only. It never deletes or changes files on the phone or in Google Drive.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no servers of its own.

Google account access

The app asks for one Google permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With it, the app can only see and change the files and folders it created itself. It cannot read anything else in the user's Drive.
