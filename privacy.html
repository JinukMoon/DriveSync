Privacy Policy

ScreenSync (Android app) · Last updated: November 25, 2026

Summary

ScreenSync moves the user's own screenshots from their phone to their own Google Drive. The developer does not receive, store, or see any of this data.

Data the app accesses

  • Photos and videos in the folder the user chooses (by default DCIM/Screenshots). They are read on the phone only to upload them.
  • Google Drive, limited to files the app created (drive.file scope). The app creates one folder and uploads files into it. It cannot read other Drive content.
  • The Google account e-mail address, shown in the app so the user can see which account is in use.

Where data goes

  • Files go directly from the phone to Google Drive over Google's encrypted API (HTTPS). They do not pass through any other server.
  • Settings and upload history (file names and sizes) are stored only on the phone, in the app's private storage.
  • Google sign-in is handled by Google Play services. The app does not store passwords or long-lived tokens.

What the app does not do

  • No ads, analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.
  • No deleting or modifying of files on the phone or in Google Drive.

Google API Services User Data Policy

ScreenSync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Drive access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uploaded files stay in the user's Drive until the user deletes them.
